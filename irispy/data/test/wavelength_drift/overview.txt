iris_l2_20140708_114109_3824262996_raster_t000_r00000_wavelength_drift_test.fits
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Cut from iris_l2_20140708_114109_3824262996_raster_t000_r00000.fits (in
2014/07/08/20140708_114109_3824262996/iris_l2_20140708_114109_3824262996_raster.tar.gz).
OBS 3824262996: Very large dense raster 131.7x175 400s, FUV spectral summing 2; its 400 steps
span 3.5 hours, 2.1 orbits.

Kept:   steps 0, 57, ..., 399 (every 57th, 8 of 400), all 1095 slit rows and three of the
        8 windows, each cut to the fit ranges of its reference lines plus 5 pixels:
          O I 1356      (extension 3) pixels 117-145 of 172, 1355.29-1356.01 A: O I
          Si IV 1394    (extension 4) pixels  62-91  of 209, 1392.47-1393.21 A: Fe II
          Mg II k 2796  (extension 8) pixels 244-504 of 537, 2799.20-2805.82 A: Ni I, Mn I, Fe I
        Indices are 0-based. The data values (raw int16 with BSCALE/BZERO) are unchanged, and
        the auxiliary and level 1 filename tables keep the same 8 rows. IDL's Ni I shifts at
        these steps span 34 mA.
Header: NWIN = 3 and the kept windows' keywords are numbered 1-3 in file order. NAXIS1/3,
        CRPIX1 and TWMINn/TWMAXn describe the cut, and CDELT3/CRPIX3 the stride of 57 steps,
        as ../compress.py decimates. NEXP = NRASTERP = 8 and STEPS_AV/STEPT_AV are 57 times
        the whole file's; DATE_OBS and DATE_END still hold, as the first and last steps are
        kept. The filename table declares TFIELDS = 9 for its 9 columns (the whole file says 7,
        see ../compress.py). TSR/TER/TSC/TEC, STEPS_DV/STEPT_DV and the statistics keywords
        (DATA*, TD*n) still describe the whole windows or file.

iris_prep_wavecorr_l2_3824262996_r00000.ecsv
        r = iris_prep_wavecorr_l2(file): one row per step in time order, with r.times, the
        shifts r.corrs of the 5 reference lines and the drifts r.corr_nuv and r.corr_fuv, in A.
        For 4000005156, IDL keeps the step without a shift (one for Ni I, one for O I) in
        the drift fits, so MPFIT fails and both drifts are its starting guess,
