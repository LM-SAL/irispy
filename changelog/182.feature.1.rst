Spectrograph cubes, including sit-and-stare observations, now have a gWCS built from the pointing, roll and time of every exposure in the auxiliary table. ``cube.fits_wcs`` is the matching FITS ``-TAB`` `~astropy.wcs.WCS`, ``cube.time`` gives the exposure times (with shape (scan, step) on a combined cube), and ``cube.celestial_frame`` gives the `~sunpy.coordinates.frames.Helioprojective` frame of the observation on any slice.
