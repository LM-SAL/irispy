Added a ``celestial_frame`` property to `~irispy.spectrograph.SpectrogramCube` and `~irispy.sji.SJICube` that returns the `~sunpy.coordinates.frames.Helioprojective` frame of the observation as seen by the IRIS observer. It replaces the ``wcs_to_celestial_frame(cube.basic_wcs.celestial)`` pattern and also works on combined multi-file cubes and on any slice of a cube. Spectrograph ``fits_wcs`` objects now also carry the observation ``DATE-OBS``, so coordinates built in ``celestial_frame`` transform cleanly through them.
