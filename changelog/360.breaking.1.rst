The WCS of a spectrograph cube is now a gWCS. Its world coordinates are wavelength, helioprojective longitude and latitude, time, raster step and raster scan.
Wavelengths are now in nm instead of m, and longitude and latitude are in arcsec instead of deg, with longitude first.
``crop`` therefore takes one entry per world coordinate, such as ``[SpectralCoord(279.63, unit=u.nm), None, None, None]`` for one raster, ``cube[k]``, and the sky position, time and raster step must be given together.
The FITS ``-TAB`` WCS that the gWCS replaces is now ``cube.fits_wcs``, a list with one WCS per raster.
