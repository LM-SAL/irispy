The low-level longitudes of the gWCS of a slit-jaw, AIA or Hinode/SOT cube, which ``cube.wcs.pixel_to_world_values`` gives and ``cube.wcs.world_to_pixel_values`` takes, are now arcseconds from -648000 to 648000, the ``Tx`` of the sky position.
They were from 0 to 1296000: convert a longitude kept from before with ``(lon + 648000) % 1296000 - 648000``.
