The WCS of a spectrograph cube is now a gWCS. Its world coordinates are wavelength, helioprojective longitude and latitude, and time.
Wavelengths are now in nm instead of m, and longitude and latitude are in arcsec instead of deg, with longitude first.
``crop`` therefore takes one entry per world coordinate, such as ``[SpectralCoord(279.63, unit=u.nm), None, None]``, and the sky position and time must be given together: the two times give the exposures that the crop keeps.
The FITS ``-TAB`` WCS that the gWCS replaces is now ``cube.fits_wcs``, a list with one WCS per raster, each with the date and observer of its own file.
