`~irispy.io.spectrograph.read_spectrograph_lvl2` with ``memmap=True`` now labels a raster window's raw integer data ``DN_IRIS_FUV_UNSCALED`` or ``DN_IRIS_NUV_UNSCALED`` instead of DN, as slit-jaw reads already do, so quantities in DN no longer convert to them: `~irispy.utils.rgb.calculate_rgb` given ``vmin`` or ``vmax`` in DN on such data now raises a ``UnitConversionError`` instead of comparing DN with the raw codes. Eager reads and float memmap data keep their DN units.
