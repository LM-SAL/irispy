The analysis functions of ``irispy.utils`` and ``SJICube.to_maps`` now give the results of a ``memmap=False`` read for cubes read with ``memmap=True``. ``calculate_moments``, ``average_window``, ``calculate_red_blue_asymmetry``, ``calculate_mg_features`` and the spectral burst finders work through such a cube a chunk of exposures at a time; the others read the cube they are given into memory, so slice or crop it first. ``uncertainty=True`` now gives an uncertainty with ``memmap=True`` too.
