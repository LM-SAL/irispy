``SJICube.to_maps`` now builds each map from its frame's FITS header, so slit-jaw maps are ``SJIMap`` and AIA maps ``AIAMap``, with the cube's unit and the frame's own exposure time, instead of ``GenericMap`` with no unit and the file's ``EXPTIME``. Frames requested out of time order, such as ``to_maps([-1, 0])``, now come back in that order with their own times.
