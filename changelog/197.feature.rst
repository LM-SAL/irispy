Added `~irispy.utils.bursts.find_bright_spectral_events` and `~irispy.utils.bursts.find_bright_image_events` to find compact bright events in the spectra of any line and in slit-jaw or IRIS-aligned AIA images, and `~irispy.utils.bursts.find_si_iv_bursts` and `~irispy.utils.bursts.find_sji_bursts` to find UV bursts in Si IV spectra and 1400 Å slit-jaw images, ported from the SolarSoft routines ``iris_burst_check`` and ``iris_sji_burst_check``.
