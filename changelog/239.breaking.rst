The level 2 readers now mask the samples at the level 2 ceiling, ``irispy.utils.constants.SATURATION_LIMIT``, keep their values and mark them in ``meta["saturated"]``. ``irispy.utils.moments.calculate_moments`` and ``irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry`` no longer take ``saturation_limit`` and use these marks instead: where a sample they use is saturated, ``calculate_moments`` gives NaN and flags the pixel in a new ``"saturated"`` map, and ``calculate_red_blue_asymmetry`` flags it ``SATURATED``.
