The ``spectral_dispersion`` and ``solid_angle`` of a rebinned spectrograph cube, and so its `~irispy.utils.spectrograph.radiometric_calibration`, now use the native pixel scales. `~ndcube.NDCube.rebin` averages pixels, so the binned pixel scales used before made calibrated intensities too low by the bin factor. `~irispy.utils.spectrograph.radiometric_calibration` also accepts a cube that is already corrected for exposure time.
