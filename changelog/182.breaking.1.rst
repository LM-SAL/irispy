`~irispy.io.read_files` and `~irispy.io.spectrograph.read_spectrograph_lvl2` now return one `~irispy.spectrograph.SpectrogramCube` per spectral window: a 3D cube for a single raster file or a sit-and-stare observation, and a 4D cube with a leading raster scan axis for a multi-file observation. ``SpectrogramCubeSequence`` and ``IRISSequencePlotter`` have been removed. On a single-file cube, ``raster[window][0]`` is now the first raster step or exposure (a 2D slit and wavelength image), not the whole raster; use `~irispy.spectrograph.SpectrogramCube.raster_slice` and `~irispy.spectrograph.SpectrogramCube.split_rasters` to get individual rasters.
