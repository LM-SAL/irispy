Reading multi-file raster spectrograph observations now returns a single `~irispy.spectrograph.SpectrogramCube` per spectral window, including in ``memmap=True`` mode.
``SpectrogramCubeSequence`` has been removed.

The combined cube exposes raster-grouping helpers through ``raster_slice()`` and ``split_rasters()``.
Cubes read with ``memmap=True`` now have ``mask=None``; derive bad-pixel masks from the unscaled data when needed.
