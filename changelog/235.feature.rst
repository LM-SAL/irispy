The level 2 and mosaic readers set the samples clipped at the level 2 ceiling, ``irispy.utils.constants.SATURATION_LIMIT``, to ``+inf``, the saturation flag ``iris_prep`` gives them and the level 2 writer loses. `~irispy.utils.moments.calculate_moments`, `~irispy.utils.mg_features.calculate_mg_features` and `~irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry` flag the pixels with such a sample in every call, and ``+inf`` survives `~irispy.utils.spectrograph.subtract_background`, exposure time correction and calibration, so a background subtracted first can no longer hide saturated samples. `~irispy.utils.moments.average_window` masks such a pixel, the burst finders count the sample as bright, and cosmic ray removal, dust removal, wavelength drift, false-color images and the fitting starts leave it out like NaN.
