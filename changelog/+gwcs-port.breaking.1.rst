The WCS of a spectrograph cube is now a gWCS whose world coordinates are wavelength, helioprojective longitude and latitude, time, raster step and raster scan; its wavelengths are in nm instead of m, and its longitude and latitude, now in that order, in arcsec instead of deg.
So ``crop`` takes one entry per world coordinate, such as ``[SpectralCoord(279.63, unit=u.nm), None, None, None]`` on one raster, ``cube[k]``, and the sky position, time and raster step must be given together; the FITS ``-TAB`` WCS that it replaces is ``cube[k].fits_wcs``.
