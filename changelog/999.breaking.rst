Reading multi-file raster spectrograph observations now returns a single
`~irispy.spectrograph.SpectrogramCube` per spectral window, including in
``memmap=True`` mode. The combined cube exposes raster-grouping helpers through
``raster_boundaries``, ``raster_slice()``, and ``split_rasters()``. The public
`~irispy.spectrograph.SpectrogramCubeSequence` compatibility type has been
removed.
