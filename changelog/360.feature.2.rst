Raster plots now draw a raster map, such as ``cube[0, :, :, 10]``, with the raster steps along x, and an image along the raster axis fills the axes.
They label the raster step and scan sliders, put latitude on the edge of the slit axis and longitude on the edge of the step axis, and show the time, raster step and raster scan coordinates only when ``axes_coordinates`` asks for them.
